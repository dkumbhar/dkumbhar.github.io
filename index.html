About

I'm Deepak Kumbhar, an Associate Partner and Hyperscaler AI Leader focused on enterprise AI, architecture, and technology transformation. Over more than 25 years, I've worked across enterprise and solution architecture, application modernization, integration, cloud, data, and increasingly AI.

Today, much of my work centers on a deceptively difficult question:

How do enterprises move AI from experimentation into production?

Answering it means looking beyond models, to architecture, context, agents, data, integration, governance, security, operating models, human adoption, and the realities of existing enterprise technology estates.

This site is where I explore those questions and share practical perspectives from that journey.

What I'm exploring

AI is making software easier to create. That does not necessarily make enterprise systems easier to build.

As models become more capable, some of the hardest problems are moving elsewhere: context, orchestration, integration, evaluation, governance, security, operating models, and deciding where humans belong in the loop.

Those are the questions I am interested in. I write about the architectural and organizational choices that determine whether AI remains an impressive prototype or becomes a dependable enterprise capability.
